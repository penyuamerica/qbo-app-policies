Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 5, 2026

Claude Accounting Assist is a private, internal integration used solely by Ninja Trading LLC to read and correct its own QuickBooks Online journal entries. It is not distributed to, or used by, any other individual or organization.

Data we access

The application accesses accounting data (journal entries, chart of accounts, and related transactions) within the Ninja Trading LLC QuickBooks Online company file, via the QuickBooks Online Accounting API, solely to identify and correct bookkeeping errors.

Data we do not do

We do not sell, share, or transmit this data to any third party. We do not use the data for advertising or profiling. Data is not retained outside of the QuickBooks Online company file and the operator's own local machine.
